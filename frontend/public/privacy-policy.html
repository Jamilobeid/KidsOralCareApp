1. Who operates eSmile

eSmile is operated by Jamil Obeid in Beirut, Lebanon. The app is designed for children ages 4–12. A parent or legal guardian must create and control the account, receive notices, provide required consent, and manage the child’s information.

2. Information collected

Parent or guardian information

  • Parent email address, Firebase account identifier, and authentication/security metadata.
  • Account creation, verification, sign-in, update, password-reset, and email-verification status.
  • Parental-consent status, method, notice version, and timestamps when implemented.

Passwords are processed by Firebase Authentication and are not stored in readable form in the application database.

Child profile and activity

  • Child-selected username or nickname, age, avatar, theme, and tooth buddy.
  • Brushing sessions, streaks, weekly and lifetime totals, and reminder-following.
  • Game activity, rewards, badges, points, levels, challenges, and app activity needed for features.
  • Reminder preferences and locally scheduled notifications.

Parents should help children choose a username that does not reveal a real name, school, location, email, telephone number, or other identifying information.

3. Use, leaderboard, and providers

Information is used to secure the account and provide brushing, game, reward, personalization, reminder, progress, parent-dashboard, recovery, support, safety, and deletion features. Leaderboard participation is optional and requires separate approved parent consent; only a non-identifying username, avatar, points or stars, level, and rank are intended to appear.

Google Firebase provides authentication, database, and security infrastructure. Netlify hosts secure backend functions and public legal information. Expo and the device operating system support builds and local reminders. eSmile does not sell child information or use targeted advertising.

4. Retention

  • Active account information is kept while needed for the service.
  • Accounts may be deleted after two years of inactivity following advance notices.
  • Detailed activity is retained for no more than two years under this policy.
  • Identifiable operational logs and transactional email queue content are retained for no more than 30 days.
  • Encrypted rolling backups expire within 30 days after active-system deletion.

5. Parent rights and deletion

A parent may request access, correction, consent withdrawal, leaderboard withdrawal, or deletion through the protected Parent Zone or by emailing jamilworkinfo@gmail.com. Recent authentication or control of the registered parent email is verified first. Verified deletion requests are normally completed in active systems within 30 days. Do not send a password by email. See Account & Data Deletion.

6. Security and changes

Measures include verified parent emails, role-based rules, protected administrator claims, recent password reauthentication for deletion, restricted service credentials, encrypted connections, and access limitations. No method is completely secure. Material changes will receive appropriate notice and renewed acceptance or consent when required.
