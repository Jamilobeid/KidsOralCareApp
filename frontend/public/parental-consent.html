Parental Consent Information

Effective date: [TO BE ADDED AT PUBLICATION]

Parent action required

eSmile is designed for children ages 4–12. A parent or legal guardian must create and control the account, verify the parent email, receive the direct notice, and complete the approved consent process before a child profile is created.

Information covered

  • The child’s chosen username, age, avatar, theme, and tooth buddy.
  • Brushing completions, times, streaks, totals, and reminder-following.
  • Game activity, rewards, badges, points, levels, challenges, and app usage.
  • Account identifiers, security information, and timestamps needed to operate and protect the service.
  • The parent email for authentication, verification, recovery, notices, and privacy requests.

How it is used

The information is used to provide the child account, brushing activities, games, rewards, personalization, reminders, parent dashboard, security, support, and deletion. eSmile does not sell this information or use it for targeted advertising.

Separate choices

Required internal processing, optional leaderboard disclosure, and optional device notifications are separate choices. Leaderboard participation is not automatic; declining it does not prevent use of core brushing, game, reward, or parent-dashboard features. A participating profile is intended to show only a non-identifying username, avatar, points, and level.

Retention and parent rights

Verified deletion requests are completed from active systems within 30 days. Detailed activity is retained for no more than two years, identifiable operational logs for no more than 30 days, and encrypted backups for a rolling maximum of 30 days under this policy. A parent may review or correct information, refuse further collection, withdraw consent, request leaderboard removal, or request deletion.
