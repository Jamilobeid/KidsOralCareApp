Account & Data Deletion

Effective date: [TO BE ADDED AT PUBLICATION]

eSmile is operated by Jamil Obeid. A parent or guardian can request permanent deletion of the family account and associated child data inside the app or by email.

Deletion is permanent

After deletion is completed, the account cannot be recovered. The child’s username may become available for another account.

Delete from the app

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Unlock the protected Parent Zone.
  3. Choose Delete account and data.
  4. Enter the parent password and type DELETE.
  5. Confirm permanent deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email jamilworkinfo@gmail.com from the parent email connected to the account with the subject “eSmile account deletion request.” Do not send any password. We may request limited information to verify control of the registered parent email.

Request account deletion

Information deleted

  • Firebase Authentication account and parent profile.
  • Child username, age, profile, preferences, and personalization.
  • Brushing, game, reward, activity, usage, and reminder-following records.
  • Username reservation and leaderboard entry.
  • Associated recovery information and active notification schedules under the app’s control.

Timing and limited retention

Verified requests are normally completed in active systems within 30 days. Encrypted rolling backups expire within 30 days after active-system deletion. Minimal non-identifying evidence of the request and completion may be retained where legally permitted or required. See the Privacy Policy for the full retention schedule.
